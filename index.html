Summary (English)

Nebi Kaban is an independent developer building screen-reader-first Android apps for blind and visually impaired users.

  • Postacı: an accessible email app modeled on iPhone Mail (privacy policy).
  • Erişilebilir SMS: an accessible SMS app. The privacy policy will soon be moved to this site. Get it on Google Play
